# Competitive intelligence program

Standing quarterly teardown (intern-runnable, ~2 days/quarter) + ad-hoc alerts.
Deliverable: one dated delta memo per quarter in this folder — new features,
stack changes, customer wins/losses, pricing intel, with source URLs.

**Methods — all public and legitimate. Never misrepresentation: no fake sales
calls, no trials under false identity, nothing outside a vendor's ToS.**

1. **Demo videos** — Finovate/conference demos, webinars, YouTube product
   tours; frame-by-frame feature inventory.
2. **Docs & changelogs** — public docs sites, release notes, status pages.
3. **Job postings** — stack signals ("Textract", "LangChain") and roadmap
   signals ("hiring E-Tran integration engineer").
4. **SBA FOIA loan-level data** (data.sba.gov) — quarterly volume/mix shifts at
   known competitor-deployed banks (Casca: Live Oak, Huntington, Celtic,
   Bankwell). The only public _outcome_ signal of whether a competitor's AI
   moves real throughput.
5. **Review sites & procurement records** — G2/Capterra, public RFPs, Vendr.
6. **Patents/trademarks** — USPTO filings.
7. **Design-partner intel** — what competitors pitched our pilot banks
   (ordinary sales intelligence; document it here).
8. **Accuracy benchmarking** — where legitimate self-serve trials exist (e.g.
   Ocrolus), run OUR golden corpus through them under their ToS, score with OUR
   scorer, publish the honest bake-off. Closed products can't be measured from
   outside — which is the point: our published, reproducible scorecard is the
   asset closed competitors cannot match.
