## Market structure (FY2025, official SBA data — computed from data.sba.gov)

- 78,078 loans / $37.29B (record). Avg ticket $477K (FY26 YTD: $535K).
- **1,426 active lenders; median = 5 loans/yr.** ≥100 loans: 92 lenders.
  ≥25: 271. Top-100 carry ~79% of loans.
- **~584 lenders used PLP delegation, carrying 64% of loan count** → natural
  first platform buyers.
- FY2026 running **-33% count YoY** (43-day shutdown froze ~$5.3B; SOP 50 10 8
  tightening killed small-loan count: ≤$150K share 54%→46% in 2 years) →
  hybrid platform+usage pricing hedges policy volatility.
- SOP 50 10 8 (eff. 2025-06-01): full underwriting required down to $50K,
  collateral above $50K, SBSS 155→165, 10% equity injection restored — more
  underwriting work per file, exactly our wedge.
