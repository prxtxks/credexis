# Pricing strategy (v1 — hypothesis to validate with design partners)

Grounded in docs/competitive/2026-Q3-baseline.md (verified benchmarks). Every
number below is a hypothesis with good anchors, not a validated price — the
design-partner conversations are the test. If nobody flinches at $45K, raise it.

## Principles

1. **The value metric is the deal file** — not pages, not seats. Matches how
   brokers, LSPs, and banks already think.
2. **Lineage/audit features ship in EVERY tier.** Traceability is the moat —
   never paywall trust.
3. **No %-of-loan success fees.** SBA rules require lender-paid, non-contingent
   fees (13 CFR 103/120.221) — and success fees make us a service, not software.
4. **Platform base + included files** hedges policy volatility (FY26 volume
   -33% YoY on shutdown + rule changes; pure per-deal revenue is hostage to
   Washington).

## Structure

**Motion 1 — Design partners (MVP 2):** 2–3 lenders/brokers at $10–15K/yr,
locked 2 years, for case-study rights + feedback access. Logos over revenue.

**Motion 2 — Banks (platform + included files):**

| Tier       | Who (market count)        | Price       | Includes                   | Effective $/file |
| ---------- | ------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Community  | ≤25 loans/yr (~270 banks) | $15K/yr     | 25 files                   | $600             |
| Growth     | ≤100 loans/yr (~90 banks) | $45K/yr     | 100 files                  | $450             |
| PLP / Head | 100–500+ (~90 banks)      | $90–150K/yr | quota + ~$125/file overage | ~$250            |

Anchors: always a fraction of the $3–4K+ manual labor per file; under the
Ocrolus median ($98K) and nCino blended (~$185K); under one loaded underwriter
salary at every tier. The ~584 actively-delegated PLP lenders (64% of files)
are the first platform targets. Marginal cost ~$0.25–1/file → >99% gross margin.

**Motion 3 — The tail via channels:** white-label to LSPs at ~$75–100/file
(they resell into the 1,000+ low-volume banks they already serve); brokers/
packagers self-serve at **~$250/deal file** (vs their $2.5–5K packaging fee).
Both are land-and-expand funnels into platform contracts.

## Positioning line

Casca sells speed to the giants on small loans. We sell **certified, auditable
underwriting capacity on full-size 7(a) files** — keep your LOS, add the audit
trail your regulators ask about, for less than a tenth of an analyst.
